# ESA Blueshell

The association's website: membership administration for the board, and public pages for
members. This glossary covers the language the code reasons in, not how it is built.

## Contributions

**Contribution period**:
A stretch of time a member owes one contribution for, with its own fees.
_Avoid_: Year, season, term

**Contribution**:
What one member owes for one contribution period, and whether it is paid.
_Avoid_: Fee, dues, invoice, debt

**Fee**:
An amount a contribution period charges, chosen by how long the membership runs into it.
_Avoid_: Price, rate, tariff

## Payment emails

**Ask**:
One recorded occasion of telling a member about money, whatever the wording. The umbrella
term: every reminder and every notification is an ask, and so is the one sent on signing up.
_Avoid_: Contact, touch, send, mail

**Reminder**:
An ask that requests a transfer by a date. Sent to a member with no direct-debit mandate.
_Avoid_: Chase, dunning, invoice, notice

**Incasso notification**:
An ask that states what will be collected, and when. Requests nothing, because the money is
taken anyway. Sent to a member with a direct-debit mandate.
_Avoid_: Debit reminder, pre-notice, mandate email

**Joining ask**:
The ask a member receives on signing up, worded as a welcome. A reminder by record, not by
sentence.
_Avoid_: Welcome email, onboarding email

**Duplicate**:
A second ask of the same kind, to the same member, for the same contribution period. Two asks
of different kinds are not duplicates of each other.

## Selecting members

**Selection**:
The members a board member has picked in the member list, before any judgement is applied.

**Disposition**:
What one send would do to one selected member: written to, warned about, or excluded.

**Warning**:
A disposition the board member may overrule. Starts unticked, so doing nothing is the safe
choice.
_Avoid_: Soft exclusion

**Exclusion**:
A disposition nobody can overrule, because the member cannot or must not be written to.
_Avoid_: Hard block, skip

**Forcibly included**:
A warned member the board member has ticked anyway.
