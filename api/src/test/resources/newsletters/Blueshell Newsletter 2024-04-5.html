Esports

The Second split has been going on for a while. Lets see how our teams are doing.

 

Rocket League

Blueshell Turtles 

The Turtles had a rough start this spilt with their opening match being a loss against our own Blueshell squirtles. In their last match they managed to get a win tho! They might still be able to bring it back and get into playoffs if they keep it up.

BS Oogway

Oogway is known to be our best RL team and they have been fighting the odds in main dsl. Here, while they had a slow start, they managed to get their first win earlier this week. Lets wish them the best and see if they can make this their turning point in the Dutch Student Leagues.

BS Squirtles

Squirtles has been demolishing some of the other teams in division 1. (they also lost 1 tho). They might even be able to reach the top of the division.  

 

League of Legends 

BS Scaling

Quite in contrast with their name, they started the season of well, but have been dropping in the ranks. Currently one opponent is between them and playoffs. Next monday we will be able to see if scaling can beat the best team in the division and get into playoffs.

BS Happy Hour

Happy Hour ended up at the bottom of division 3. They have already played all their games and it seems like their best efforts were just too short of being enough. With a lot of ties and a few losses under their name I would like to say that they played some great games. Maybe we will see them again next split (or in a demotion bracket if there is one)

 

 

Valorant

Bs Tempra

They lost to ETT :(... Over the past few weeks Tempra has been going up against some of the best teams in division 2 without too much success. Lets see if they can turn this around in coming games

IcySpicy

With 0 losses under their name, IcySpicy has been blasting their way through division 3. With their current games they might be able to become the top team of the division. 

WaterBoarders

It would seem that we have become the worst valorant team in the lowest division of DCVS. We will be bringing it back next month tho :) (this might be a lie)

 

CS2

HyperS

HyperS promoted into division 1 last split and they have been rolling over the opposition. They are currently at the top of division 1 with only a single loss under their name. Lets see if they can keep it up. (ofcourse they also beat ETT again)
