Esports

The Second split is coming to an end. Playoffs are still going strong. Lets see how our teams are doing

 

Rocket League

Blueshell Turtles 

The Turtles came close, but barely did not make it into playoffs. They did well this split and I hope to see them back next year.

BS Oogway

Oogway finished their adventure in dsl going with 2 wins and 6 losses. They fought hard, but did not make it into main DSL playoffs. To you guys I wish the best and hope to see you back next year.

BS Squirtles

Squirtles barely made it into playoffs of division one where they will be playing against some of the best teams in the coming weeks. Come cheer them on as they play their last games of the year!

 

League of Legends 

BS Scaling

After losing their final match against the strongest team in division 3 they did not make it into playoffs. While these old men did not make it this split, they still deserve some respect for having everyone play offrole, after promoting to a higher division. 

BS Happy Hour

Happy Hour Did not play last month. 

 

 

Valorant

Bs Tempra

Sadly, they did not manage to turn it around. With a solid score of 2 wins 2 ties 3 losses, our best Valorant team ended the season not making it into the playoffs of division 2. 

IcySpicy

Icyspicy is still going strong! They are a team with 2 of our current kandi in it and made it into playoffs of division 3 with a solid score. I hope they can keep winning the rest of their games this split.

WaterBoarders

Lost to IcySpicy and ended on the bottom of div 3 :(

 

CS2

HyperS

I asked Jonas how HyperS was doing. He did not know, so I also don't know. It seems like they made it into playoffs of division 1 tho. Keep it up!
