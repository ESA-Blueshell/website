Newsletter
December 2024

Dear members,
 
The second module of the year has gotten well underway and with it many new events have taken place in our association. Among these we had some hugely successful in person ones that lead a great number of new members to join us! I will now give you a brief recap on what happened over the past month in Blueshell and what events are upcoming over the next month.

 

Sincerely, 
Joris Jonkers

Secretary & External Affairs of E.S.A. Blueshell
­

December in Blueshell

Over november we had several large in person events both in the  Predator Esports Lounge and abroad! These events drew in a lot of new members and we heard nothing but positive feedback on hosting more such events.
­

La Cubanita Dinner with 4FunCie

At the La Cubanita dinner we got a chance to chill and have some great food together with the rest of our lovely association members. We were happy to see that all the spots for this activity filled up rapidly and will aim to host more of these in person events as everyone had a great time together :)
­

Blocky Sculptures!

One of our many physical events was the Block Sculptures! member initiative event organized by Sanne! Our great former external got together the most creative minds of our association and I'm told everybody had a great time :) 
If you are reading this and have a great idea for organizing such an event, please reach out to our lovely internal Yannick Sloot on discord, or contact us via the board questions discord channel 💗
­

4FunCie Münster Christmas Market

On the final day of the month the 4FunCie hosted a christmas market trip for the members of our association, we counted a grand total of 8 brand new members that joined us for this event, great job 4FunCie! Everybody had a great time, drinking Glühwein, eating bratwurst (and apparently some amazing Döner), and socializing with their fellow Blueshell members.
If you should be interested in organizing amazing events like this, please reach out to one of our committees or directly to the board in the board questions
­

Esports Update

Blueshell has really rallied over the past month and managed to participate in many of the DSL tournaments! Due to miscalculation in the ranking, we are uncertain whether Blueshell is first or second place in the 2024 Fall Split for the Tournament Series, but we will make sure to push through and get the guaranteed first spot! 
Thanks all for keeping an eye on the DSL announcements channel and making sure that we remain at the top of the Fall Split rankings :)

Upcoming in January 

The last month of the year has now begun and that means that our fair association will see a slight slowdown of events due to the long winter break. But, don't worry! We have some great events lined up for you to do some holiday bonding before you'll have to miss all your friends in the association for the long winter months. 
­

Events

For The last month of the year we have a great many events lined up, some of which to help get into the Christmas spirit with your fellow Blueshell members, and yet others to introduce other students to all the amazing things our association has to offer through some great collaborative events. For more information you can have a look at the events info channel.
