# ADR-036: A Membership Is Pending Until Its First Contribution

## Status
Accepted

## Context

The **member** role follows an active membership, and a membership is active from the day
signing up starts it. Somebody who signs up and never pays is a member everywhere the role
reaches: members-only events, and soon the Discord Member role that decides who may write in
the games channels.

The association wants membership to mean somebody paid at least once. It does not want a
member to drop out at every period rollover while the treasurer is still chasing, nor a
grace date to maintain each year.

## Decision

**A membership is pending until a contribution for it is paid, and active from then until
the board ends it.**

- Signing up starts a pending membership. An honorary membership is active from the start.
- Regular and alumni memberships need the first contribution; later periods do not gate
  the role. Chasing a later period is the treasurer's business, and ending a membership is
  the board's.
- Rejoining after a membership ended starts a new pending membership.
- The member role follows an active membership, as before, so every check that reads the
  role keeps its meaning.

Alternatives considered:

- **Member only while this period is paid.** Every rollover would strip the role from
  everyone until they pay, with a grace date to set each year.
- **A "has ever paid" fact on the person.** It would outlive the membership it was paid for,
  so a returning member would be a member before paying again.

## Consequences

- Every membership active on the day this lands stays active, paid or not; only memberships
  started afterwards begin pending.
- Paying what the **first payment email** asks is now what turns a membership on, so that
  payment must be recorded promptly for the new member to get in.
