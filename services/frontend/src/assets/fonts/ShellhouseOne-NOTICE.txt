Shellhouse One
==============

A modified version of Fugaz One by Daniel Hernandez (LatinoType), with the six Turkish
characters the original does not carry added to it:

    U+0130 İ    U+0131 ı    U+011E Ğ    U+011F ğ    U+015E Ş    U+015F ş

The added characters are composed from the original's own outlines. Nothing else was changed.

The original is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, and so is this: the
licence is beside this file as ShellhouseOne-OFL.txt, and the original copyright notice and
Reserved Font Names are kept in it and in the font's own name table.

Renamed because the licence says to. "Fugaz" and "Fugaz One" are Reserved Font Names, and
clause 3 of the OFL is that no modified version may use them as the name it presents to
users. The font's family, full and PostScript names are Shellhouse One, and its manufacturer
is Blueshell; the designer credit stays with Daniel Hernandez, whose outlines these are.

Neither LatinoType nor Daniel Hernandez is affiliated with this version, and neither has
endorsed it.
