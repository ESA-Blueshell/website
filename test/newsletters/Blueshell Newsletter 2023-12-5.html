Esports

The first split is coming to an end. Playoffs are already happening in most games. Lets see how our teams did in the past month.

 

Rocket League

Blueshell Turtles 

Even though BS Turtles lost their first match this month. They are still finished miles above the rest of the competition in terms of points (3 points (1 win)). Now for their next match. The first game of playoffs this team will be facing my favorite team(no bias...): BS Tadpoles.

BS Oogway

As the strongest div 1 team of Blueshell Bs Oogway has been playing on the level of the best teams in the division. Having made it into playoffs, I am looking forward to see how far they can kick it.

BS Squirtles

Sadly they have not brought it back as we hoping for last week. According to captain Thomas it is due to the lack of Rocket league they have played recently. Both as a team and individually. They can not make it into playoffs anymore this split. But we might see them make a comeback in the next one. (Edit: They won their first game today. Great job guys!)

BS Tadpoles

Winning! The past 2 games were not the best for Tadpoles. Yet with the great games they have played earlier in the season, they have made it into Playoffs. So wish them luck as they face BS Turtles in their coming match.

 

League of Legends 

BS Scaling

This team has been demolishing all of their competition. Having already secured their place in the finals of playoffs while 0 losses the whole split. (Bob also bullied me in midlane >:0 )

 

BS Housecats

While having won everything in the previous month. This month was not purrfect for our housecats. It was a total cat-astrophe! Having tied their first match this month and lost the other 2, they still make it into playoffs placing third in their division.

 

Valorant

Bs Tempra

Lost to ETT Gamechangers (was technically this month) and TSEA Froggos :(. Since they have 2 matches to play, I believe can potentially make it into playoffs by have a 3 way tie with 3rd and 4th place in the division. So wish the Tempras the best of luck and come see if Sir Bigmonke can pop off again like he did on Ascent. (Edit: they won against Erasmus so only one more to go(Edit 2: they tied the last match and are now out.)) 

SpicyWater and IcyWater

This month was a clash between the waters! First they faced each other in the first in normal competition and the week after in playoffs! While the first game with a lot of substitudes resulted in a tie. Icywater managed to dominate in playoffs where both teams played with main roster because Spicy was a coach and Lars down. 

 

CS2

HyperS

Last month I said that HyperS would beat ETT, but they did not... They tied :(. Since then they are going as strong as ever. With 0 losses the whole competition, the rest of the split is looking quite well for this team.
