How was the last Year

2023-2024

Last year had a lot of events and these were some of my favorite moments. 

LegaCie Gigantic gamenight

Batavieren with Dorans

Disco Rythem and Dance

Other events

There were tons of amazing other events for which I am not able to find pictures that do them justice. Thus I have decided to give a little shout-out to a few of my favorites!

First and foremost I would like to mention the pokemon showdown event by NintenCo. An amazing event that was hybrid to be accessable both from the Esports Lounge and online. The first of its kind.

Next I would like to mention the murder mystery night by SacrafiCie. An amazing and original event made by some of our newer members. It was the only event this year that I regret missing out on.

Last but certainly not least is the LegaCie Escape room. An incredible amount of effort went into making this event possible a possibility and in my optinion it was all worth it. The event was amazing.

Ofcourse there were a lot of other great events besides these, but here I would like to start looking to the future. I look forward to seeing what amazing events our community will come with over the coming year!

Esports

Some The Blueshell Circuit Showdown finished. After a lot of amazing games the best Esports team of Blueshell has been decided. 

Hereby I would like to congratulate Blueshell Scaling with becoming the best team of Blueshell in the year 2023-2024. 

I would also like to thank Bob, Ivo and Jelle again for making the showdown a reality. It was an incredebly fun tourney. Thank you <3

 

DCES

The first split of DCES is about to start. A big Esports competition of students within the Netherlands. If you are interested in joining a team, please come to the scouting grounds on the 20th and 21st of september in the Predator Esports Lounge in Bastille. You can sign up via https://forms.gle/t3Qg5JWFZWy6fFjd9

 

For our existing teams. I asked a few days ago what teams will be coming back in the coming split. While I can not name with certainty for most of the teams if we will be seeing them this year. I was happy to see tho that a lot of players were excited to come back and play again another split. I hope to see all of you again next year!
