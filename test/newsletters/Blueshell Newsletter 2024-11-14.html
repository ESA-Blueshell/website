Newsletter
November 2024

Dear members,

 

The first two months of the new year have ended, and with it the kick-out period has concluded. We had many great events over the past two months and enjoyed seeing many new faces! Some of you will receive this newsletter for the first time and those I wish to sincerely welcome to our association 💗 I will now give a brief recap of what has happened in the association and about the upcoming events
 

Sincerely, 
Joris Jonkers

Secretary & External Affairs of E.S.A. Blueshell

­

September

In the first month of the new academic year we had the change GMM during which the 8th Board of our fine association was inaugurated. Besides this we also had our Kick-Off period during which we show interested students what our association is all about. And last but not least we formed the Esports teams for the upcoming season of the DCES.

­

Change GMM

On the Third of September the 8th board of our association was inaugurated at the change GMM. During this meeting the policy plan for the new board was discussed and approved, and an opportunity for our members to have input on this plan was given. After some discussion the policy plan was approved and the 8th board inaugurated.

On the left side of the image from left-to-right the members of the 8th Board of ESA Blueshell

  • Yannick Sloot - Commissioner of Internal Affairs
  • Joris Jonkers - Secretary and Commissioner of External Affairs
  • Chris Wong - Treasurer and Commissioner of Esports Affairs
  • Michal Rokita - Chair
­

Kick-Off

We started this year with the Kick-Off period in which we introduced many new and old students to our association. During this time we had a great many events which we hope you all enjoyed.

­

LegaCie Gamenight

The year started of with a bang with the reintroduction of LegaCie Gamenights!
With over 40 people showing up including many new faces. It sure was a blast being back together for a large Gamenight :)

­

DisCo Rhythm, Dance & Karaoke

The Kick-Off continued with the DisCo Rhythm, Dance & Karaoke night. All who where there enjoyed sharing their passion for music related games and of course singing. A special thanks all those that shared their beautiful voices 💗
­

4FunCie Bowling Event

Our first non-gaming event of the year was a great success. With the sign-ups filling rapidly with 29 participants. Whilst not all of us could be winners, we all won by getting to spend time with such a great group of people 😊

­

Esports Update

Our Scouting Grounds, in collaboration with ETT, has brought in many new players this year. With over 90 sign-ups across four different games, we are proud to field so many talented players in our teams. DCES has kicked off, and we are participating in this collegiate competition with a total of 13 teams. We are also excited to introduce our new Rainbow Six Siege team, BS 6Shell. Let’s get to know all of our teams!

October

In the second month of the academic year we hosted our committee market, organized our second ever Constitution Drink and had the first DCES matches. This was also the first month that our new members could start participating in events. 

­

Committee Market

Unfortunately the committee market was not that successful this year, with only a few new members showing up who were outnumbered significantly by the committee members themselves. We will of course try again at a later date, and reach out to our new members directly to see if they are interested in joining committees but didn't have an opportunity to attend.
­

Constitution Drink

This month we hosted our second even Constitution Drink in the Predator Esports Lounge (PEL). It was a HUGE SUCCESS! we had over 80 people from all kinds of study, sports and culture associations from all over the University of Twente in the PEL, and we hosted several of our sister associations from across the Netherlands. 

Honorable mentions are:

  • E.S.E.V. Zephyr from Eindhoven
  • W.S.G. Paragon from Wageningen
  • Dorans from Nijmegen
