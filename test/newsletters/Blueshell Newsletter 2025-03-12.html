Half-Year GMM

On the third of March the 8th board hosted their Half-Year GMM and we were happy to see a great number of attendants at this special occasion. The GMM was mostly spent reflecting on the past half-year and how we as a board have managed the association in this time. Key takeaways were that the board needs to refocus on running the visible parts of the association over managing the behind the scenes, and that the board will work on its event attedance and communication to our members. Unfortunately due to how long the half-year GMM took the board was unable to get approval for all documents and a second GMM will be scheduled in April to approve the Long-Term Vision 2025-2030 proposal of the ViCie committee of the association. More information on this will follow soon!

Esports Update 

This month the spring split of the DSL is starting the board is hoping to see the same level of enthusiasm as shown in the past split once more this spring. Though we understand that there were some changes to handing out college coins/tournament series points, the board hopes this won't discourage people from joining the tournaments. In fact, to tackle this, the board will try to improve this for upcoming split via:

  1. In our meeting with DSL, we discussed whether the minimum requirement could be lowered for tournaments that need teams (e.g., 5v5 tournaments were extremely hard to fulfill the quota). The board hopes that this will encourage more teams to join in DSL tournaments.
  2. New @unknown-user role. This role is obtainable in (⁠Blueshell Esports⁠), and the board will start pinging this role for any DSL tournaments or external tournaments! In fact, the board wonders whether this is enough or if it might be better for the community to also be able to ping this. If you consider picking this role up, please react to the poll down below.
  3. Better promotion for the DSL tournaments. Aside from occasionally pinging people, the board will attempt to ping the tournaments role regularly with 'decent' promotion.
